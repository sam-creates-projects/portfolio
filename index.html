Strategic Roadmap

US University Scholarship & Funding Guide

Proven 5-step playbook to secure tuition waivers and Graduate Assistantships (RA/TA)

01

GitHub Profile & Code Quality

Organize top 3-4 repositories with clean architecture, live demos, test coverage, and comprehensive READMEs. Professors review code quality when offering Research Assistantships (RA).

02

Strategic Cold Emailing

Identify 5-8 faculty members per target university whose recent papers align with your GitHub projects. Send personalized 150-word emails linking your relevant projects.

03

University Portfolio Mix

Target a balanced mix: 3 Ambitious (Tier 1), 4 Target (Tier 2 with strong departmental funding history), and 2 Safe universities known for TA/RA availability.

04

SOP & LOR Alignment

Draft your Statement of Purpose (SOP) directly connecting your technical projects to specific university labs. Secure LORs that explicitly validate your research potential.

05

Early Application Submission

Submit applications before priority scholarship deadlines (typically Dec 1 or Dec 15 for Fall admission) to enter priority funding pools.
